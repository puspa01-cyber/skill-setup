Our Story

Crafted with Passion, Baked with Fire

Since 2018, we've been perfecting the art of wood-fired pizza with 72-hour cold-fermented dough and the finest ingredients.

Wood-Fired at 900°F

Our Neapolitan hearth cooks each pizza in 90 seconds, creating the perfect leopard-spotted char.

72-Hour Dough

Cold-fermented with Caputo 00 flour, sea salt, water, and wild yeast. No shortcuts, incredible flavor.

Locally Sourced

Farm-fresh produce from Central Valley growers, hand-pulled mozzarella made daily, seasonal ingredients.
